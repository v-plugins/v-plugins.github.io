# Privacy Policy: Subscribe & Save Helper for Amazon

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Subscribe & Save Helper for Amazon ("the extension") is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon.

## What the extension stores

To show your savings and subscription count, the extension keeps the following on your own device:

- your settings (the toggles in the popup),
- each Subscribe & Save discount it applied: date, Amazon site, product title, ASIN, order number, discount percentage, amount saved and currency,
- products an order subscribed you to: date, Amazon site, product title, ASIN and order number,
- the number of active subscriptions last seen on Amazon's subscriptions page.

This data is kept in Chrome's local extension storage. It stays on your computer, is not synced to other devices, and is never sent to the developer or anyone else. At most the 500 most recent entries are kept. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## What the extension sends

The extension has no server. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code, and it never sends data to the developer or to any third party.

It only runs on Amazon pages you have open on amazon.de, amazon.com and amazon.co.uk. When enabled in the popup, it ticks Subscribe & Save checkboxes at checkout, and it cancels subscriptions on your subscriptions page when you ask it to. With "Fast opt-in" enabled, it sends Amazon the same opt-in request the checkout page itself uses, from your open checkout and with your existing Amazon session. These requests go only to Amazon and are covered by Amazon's own privacy notice.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to keep the data described above on your device.
- **Access to amazon.de, amazon.com and amazon.co.uk**: to read checkout, order confirmation and subscriptions pages, and to apply discounts and cancellations there. The extension does not run on any other website.

## Sharing and sale of data

The developer receives no user data, so none is collected, sold or shared. Use of information from Amazon pages complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
